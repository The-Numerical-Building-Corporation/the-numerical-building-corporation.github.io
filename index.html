Built on measurement

Most energy analysis is estimated from default tables: typical efficiencies, typical U‑values, typical schedules. We measure. Equipment under real operating conditions, the building's own sensors and meters, and the envelope where it matters all go into a physics model of your site.

The numbers that come out describe the building that's actually there. Use them to tune operations and to plan capital work.

Start with what you have: utility bills, interval data, drawings, audits, spec sheets. The engine turns that pile into a structured, calibrated model of your building.
